用户协议

生效日期：2026-09-28

服务内容

Nocturne 提供剧本浏览、单人案件试玩、房间创建与加入，以及房间内的游戏状态同步。部分服务依赖网络；无网络时，内置剧本的单人试玩仍可使用。

用户内容

请仅上传你拥有合法权利或已获授权的剧本、图片和文字。不得上传违法、侵权、欺诈、骚扰、仇恨、色情、恶意软件或危害未成年人的内容。发布方可以移除违规内容或关闭相关房间。

房间行为

请尊重其他玩家，不得冒充房主、干扰游戏、利用接口攻击服务或分享他人的私人信息。当前版本使用匿名访客身份；正式加入账号、语音、支付或用户生成内容前，发布方必须补充账号安全、举报、拉黑、未成年人保护和内容审核规则。

服务限制

网络中断、维护或第三方基础设施故障可能导致房间暂时不可用。剧本试玩数据和房间状态可能随着版本更新调整。

联系信息

正式商店提交前，发布方必须补充真实运营主体、客服邮箱和争议处理信息。当前项目反馈入口为 GitHub Issues。

Terms of service

Effective date: 2026-09-28

Service

Nocturne provides script discovery, solo case trials, room creation and joining, and synchronized game state inside rooms. Some services require a network connection; built-in solo cases remain playable offline.

User content

Only upload scripts, images and text that you own or are authorized to use. Do not upload unlawful, infringing, fraudulent, harassing, hateful, sexual, malicious or child-endangering content. The publisher may remove violating content or close related rooms.

Room conduct

Respect other players. Do not impersonate a host, disrupt a game, attack the service through its interfaces or share another person's private information. The current release uses anonymous guests; account security, reporting, blocking, age-safety and moderation rules must be added before accounts, voice, payments or user-generated content are introduced.

Service limitations

Network interruptions, maintenance or third-party infrastructure failures may make rooms temporarily unavailable. Script and room data may change as the product evolves.

Contact

Before a store submission, the publisher must add the real operating entity, support email and dispute process. The current project feedback channel is GitHub Issues.
